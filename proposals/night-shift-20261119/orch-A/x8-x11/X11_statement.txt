WHAT X11 MUST DELIVER (the exact model premise)
 For every model step, TS:126-136 applies next_interfer after the instruction and demands state_ok (= arm8_ok,
 AT:20-26) of the pre-, mid- and post-state. The stored premise (E4:153-154) is
   interference_ok envmc.next_interfer (arm8_target.proj envmc.prog_addresses)
 = (ENV:7-13) ∀i ms. PSTATE, SCTLR_EL1, TCR_EL1, exception, REG (all 32), PC unchanged ∧ MEM unchanged on dm.
 Under the construction of X8 §2 this becomes, per real U event: arm8_proj dm (absM r_{i+1}) = arm8_proj dm (absM q_i)
 (A-KERNEL-PRESERVE), where absM erases TBI0/TBI1/SA0 and normalises x18. Also: the instruction itself must be a
 user-mode step of the L3 subset (A-CPU-ISA) and the erasure side conditions N1–N5 hold.

 A-SIG / A-SIG-DFL / A-NO-DEBUGGER / A-NO-SHARED / A-SINGLE-THREAD (ENFORCEABLE by the launcher or TESTED-UNPROVED
 by the import list; residual UNKNOWN about libSystem/dyld internals), A-MAP-WX (UNKNOWN until T1),
 A-MAP-BACKED / A-KERN-RLIMIT / A-KERN-SCHED (safe direction: a killed or stopped process gives a prefix or nothing;
 the consumer refuses), A-CPU-ISA (see X8 §b). No row can be closed by HOL: a verified model of XNU does not exist
 in the repository or the toolchain; the L3 model treats SVC as UNDEFINED (review-EE), so every route ends at a named
 kernel assumption (TOP_LEVEL_GRAPH.md:64).
