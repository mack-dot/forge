  Strength of trace_ok. It does NOT demand that the model step equals the recorded state outright. It demands
  P (mc.target.next ms) = P s, i.e. agreement under the free projection P, plus the evaluate guards of the
  normal-step branch on (ms, next ms, s). The equality is outright only for P = identity (the toy). For X8 one
  instantiates P := arm8_target.proj dm = arm8_proj dm, and arm8_proj d s = (s.PSTATE, s.SCTLR_EL1, s.TCR_EL1,
  s.exception, s.REG, fun2set (s.MEM,d), s.PC) (arm8_targetScript.sml:365-368). So s may differ from the model
  step only in SP_EL0, the EL1-3 SP/SCTLR/TCR copies, branch_hint and memory outside dm. This is the same freedom
  as X8 v3 (E1) (UPD + proj guard). Not trivial, not over-strong; it is exactly the CakeML interference_ok slack.
  At FFI events trace_ok demands the RECORDED nb to equal the oracle answer (call_FFI ... = FFI_return st1 nb):
  this is the X8 F-event induction step (N10/N12 -> NB_k = basis_ffi answer); it is moved INTO trace_ok, not proved.
  Satisfiable for a non-trivial trace: only shown on the toy (D4). Not shown for any arm8 or refund trace.

  Clock and right-hand side. Yes: the equation is only at clock LENGTH tr + k (each event = one clock tick; TrFfi
